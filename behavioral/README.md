# Behavioral Engineering

This section captures professional experiences, leadership learnings, decision-making approaches, and lessons from real-world engineering situations.

The focus is on communicating technical ownership, impact, collaboration, and engineering judgment through meaningful experiences.

## Key Areas

- Ownership & Accountability
- Customer Obsession
- Delivering Results
- Problem Solving & Decision Making
- Handling Ambiguity
- Conflict Resolution
- Collaboration & Influence
- Technical Leadership
- Failure, Feedback & Learning
- Managing Priorities

## Experience Bank

A collection of real professional experiences documented using the STAR framework:

- **Situation:** What was the context?
- **Task:** What needed to be accomplished?
- **Action:** What actions did I take?
- **Result:** What was the outcome and impact?

## Leadership Principles

Exploring leadership principles and mapping relevant professional experiences to demonstrate engineering ownership and impact.

**Goal:** Communicate engineering decisions, leadership, ownership, and professional impact with clarity and authenticity.
